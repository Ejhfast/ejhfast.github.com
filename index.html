Current work

Building at the edge of machine learning, biology, and human systems.

I am a co-founder and CTO of Vcreate, where we build high-throughput systems for T-cell receptor discovery. Before that I co-founded Nash, helped start the open source group City of Zion, and built Proxino, a Y Combinator-backed JavaScript analytics company.

My academic work began at Stanford, where I studied human-computer interaction, machine learning, programming tools, and natural language. I still care about tools that help people think, systems that make complexity usable, and long-running questions about language and culture.
