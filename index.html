I work across startups, machine learning, biology, programming tools, and public writing. My most recent work is on Vcreate, a company I co-founded in 2020 that builds high-throughput platforms for T-cell receptor discovery; our work has led to multiple SBIR awards, patent filings, and machine learning systems for immunology.

Before that I co-founded Nash, where we use state channels and MPC to make self-custody feel closer to centralized finance UX. The platform now lets users in Europe manage traditional bank accounts and self-custodied crypto assets in one place. We also issued the first regulated European security token.

I have a PhD in Computer Science from Stanford, where I worked on human-computer interaction, language, and machine learning, including Best Paper award-winning systems and papers at CHI, EMNLP, AAAI, UIST, and Nature Biotechnology.
